# AI Opportunity Validation Kit

## Product promise

Find a narrow AI/automation problem, turn it into a paid pilot offer, and validate demand before spending days building software.

This is an execution kit for solo founders, freelancers, automation builders, and small agencies.

## What is included

1. 01-opportunity-scorecard.csv — structured scoring sheet.
2. 02-validation-checklist.md — 48-hour validation sequence.
3. 03-ai-research-prompts.md — prompts for evidence collection and offer design.
4. 04-offer-card-template.md — one-page paid pilot template.
5. 05-license-and-risk-check.md — open-source reuse/commercial-risk checklist.

## Core workflow

DISCOVER → EVIDENCE → SCORE → PACKAGE → VALIDATE PAYMENT → BUILD

The kit deliberately separates an interesting technology from a validated business opportunity.

## Who it is for

- AI automation freelancers
- Solo founders
- Indie hackers
- Small agencies
- Developers looking for productized services
- Creators who want a practical digital product instead of a generic prompt bundle

## What it does not promise

It does not promise revenue, guaranteed demand, or a guaranteed winning niche. The objective is to reduce wasted build time by forcing evidence and payment validation before larger implementation.

## Suggested product positioning

**Stop building AI products nobody asked for. Validate the problem and the paid outcome first.**
