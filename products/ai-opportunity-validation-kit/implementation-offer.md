# AI Opportunity Implementation Pilot

## Offer

Turn one repetitive business workflow into a working AI-assisted process.

This is an implementation service, not a generic consultation.

## Pilot deliverable

- workflow audit
- bottleneck map
- automation design
- AI/tool selection
- working prototype or documented implementation
- handover instructions
- one revision cycle

## Test price

**Rp250.000**

This is a launch hypothesis and should be changed using real payment and delivery evidence.

## Best-fit problems

Examples:

- repetitive lead qualification
- document or spreadsheet processing
- recurring research
- content production workflows
- customer-response drafting
- internal reporting
- data cleanup and transformation

The pilot should remain narrow enough to deliver quickly.

## Payment

Payment is handled directly through the merchant QRIS shown on the checkout page.

Do not treat a customer screenshot as payment proof. Confirm the transaction on the merchant side before starting delivery.

## Validation target

The objective of this offer is not maximum traffic. It is evidence that a specific workflow has enough economic value for someone to pay for implementation.
