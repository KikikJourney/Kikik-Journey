# AI Research Prompt Pack

## 1. Problem extraction

Analyze this repository/project and identify the 5 most concrete user problems visible in its README, issues, discussions, or documentation. For each problem, quote only the minimum necessary evidence, identify the affected user, and explain the workflow that is failing.

## 2. Buyer hypothesis

For each problem, identify the most likely paying buyer. Do not invent demographics. Use the project's actual workflow, integrations, deployment model, and issue language as evidence.

## 3. Paid outcome

Turn the strongest problem into a fixed-scope paid outcome. Specify deliverable, acceptance criteria, delivery time, dependencies, and exclusions.

## 4. Productization

Identify which parts of the paid pilot repeat across customers. Separate reusable process from customer-specific work.

## 5. Competitive gap

Compare the current workflow with the proposed outcome. Identify exactly one friction point that can be removed without rebuilding the entire upstream product.

## 6. Validation

Design a 48-hour test that can distinguish real buying intent from curiosity. Prefer a paid pilot, pre-order, deposit, or explicit implementation commitment over vanity metrics.

## 7. License review

Given the repository license and the proposed use, list what must be checked before redistribution, hosted use, modification, or commercial delivery. Do not assume that "open source" means unrestricted commercial reuse.
