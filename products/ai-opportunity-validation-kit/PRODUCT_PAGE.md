# AI Opportunity Validation Kit — Product Page

## Headline

**Stop building AI products nobody asked for. Validate the paid outcome first.**

## One-line promise

A compact execution kit for solo founders and AI builders who want to turn a real workflow problem into a paid pilot before investing in a larger product.

## What the buyer gets

- Opportunity scorecard
- 48-hour validation checklist
- AI research prompt pack
- One-page paid pilot template
- License and commercial-risk checklist

## Who it is for

- Solo AI builders
- Automation freelancers
- Indie hackers
- Small agencies
- Developers exploring micro-SaaS ideas
- Creators who prefer practical systems over generic prompt collections

## The workflow

**DISCOVER → EVIDENCE → SCORE → PACKAGE → VALIDATE PAYMENT → BUILD**

The kit is intentionally outcome-first. It helps the buyer decide what to test, what to sell, and what not to build yet.

## Launch price hypothesis

**US$19**

This is a test price, not a claim about market value. The price should be revised from actual purchase and refund behavior.

## What makes it different

The kit does not sell a giant list of AI ideas. It forces each opportunity through evidence, buyer clarity, fixed deliverables, validation, and license review.

## Delivery

Instant digital download containing the complete template pack.

## Important note

No revenue outcome is guaranteed. Third-party projects, licenses, APIs, and dependencies must be reviewed independently before commercial reuse.
