How ordering works

  1. Choose an offer and enter your contact email.
  2. Pay using USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20), the only payment method currently supported by automated checkout.
  3. Submit the order request and follow the payment instructions.
  4. Payment is independently verified before the order becomes PAID.
  5. After verification, confirmation/delivery is sent to the supplied email.

Automated checkout currently supports USDT on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) only. QRIS and DANA are not available in this checkout until an independent merchant-side verification process is implemented. No passwords, OTPs, API keys, seed phrases, private keys or full credentials are requested.
